Aeolis.jl

A Julia port of AeoLiS (de Vries et al., 2023), the process-based model of aeolian sediment transport with spatially and temporally varying sediment availability (Hoonhout and de Vries, 2016).

A simulation is an AeolisProblem of AeolisParameters, solved with the SciML interface:

using Aeolis
p = read_config("aeolis.txt")          # or AeolisParameters(; xgrid_file=..., ...)
sol = solve(AeolisProblem(p), EulerBackward(Trunk()))
sol[:zb]                                # bed level at every output time

The model reproduces aeolis-python 3.1.0rc1 to round-off; behaviours of AeoLiS that look unintended are kept so that results agree, and documented where they occur.

Array layout

Spatial arrays are (nx+1, ny+1), with layers and fractions after them: x is the first dimension. A transect (a vector xgrid_file) becomes a grid of three identical rows, as in AeoLiS; transect returns the middle row of saved output.
